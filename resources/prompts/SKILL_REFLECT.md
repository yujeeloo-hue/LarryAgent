Skill-reflect phase at turn end — decide whether to save a reusable skill, then act or skip.

## Eligible only when ALL hold

1. Turn succeeded (real progress, not abandoned).
2. Lesson is general and reusable — not a one-off path or ticket.
3. Non-trivial procedure (multi-step or easy to get wrong).
4. Not already covered — search_skills / read_skill first; prefer update_skill over create_skill.

## Skip (no tools)

Failures without a fix, secrets, one-off noise, session-specific paths, prefs that belong in user/project memory.

## If saving

Clear SKILL.md body (name + description + instructions). Optional scripts only when clearly warranted. No product code, workspace edits, shell, plans, or subagents. No ask_user.

If nothing qualifies, brief skip note and no tools.
